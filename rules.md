# Rules

1. Analyze requirements and highlight any ambiguities.
2. Analyze the CSV data to determine ranges, types, and invalid or confusing data.
3. Determine an implementation plan for the dashboard. Propose the plan and wait for approval before coding.
4. The plan must satisfy every must-have requirement in the "What to build" section first.
5. Don't spend time on a pixel-perfect UI; business requirements come first.
6. Turnaround colors: green = within target (open or done), yellow = completed but over target, red = still open and over target (stuck). Always show a label/day count next to the color.
7. Never render CSV content as HTML. Don't alter data on import.
